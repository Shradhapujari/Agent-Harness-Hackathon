PART 1

🏢 What is a data center?

Imagine a HUGE room full of computer shelves called racks. These computers run apps for lots of people, like YouTube or games. They get hot, so big fans called CRAC units blow cold air on them, like an air conditioner for robots.

PART 2

😱 The problem: 40 alarms, but only ONE boo-boo

When ONE fan breaks, it gets hot. Hot makes computers panic. Then EVERYTHING starts beeping at once — like when one kid falls down and 40 people scream! A tired human has to figure out it's really just ONE problem.

PART 4

🙋 The most important rule: ASK FIRST!

The robot can do SAFE things by itself. But for big scary things — like turning a computer OFF — it must ask a human "is this okay?" first. Just like you ask a grown-up before doing something big!

✅ Safe stuff — robot just does it
Move apps to a cooler rack
🙋 Big stuff — robot asks a human first
Turn off / power-cycle a computer
